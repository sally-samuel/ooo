Our Solutions

Indigenous Solar Panels

TÜV-certified solar panels locally manufactured in Lagos, combining world-class technology, faster availability, reliable warranty support, and performance built for Nigerian conditions.

Hybrid Solar Systems

Enjoy reliable power day and night with intelligent systems that seamlessly combine solar, batteries, the grid, and generators for maximum uptime and energy savings.

PV Grid-Tied Systems

Lower your electricity bills without the cost of large battery banks by harnessing solar energy during business hours for daytime usage when your facility needs it most.

Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)

Store power from solar, grid, and other sources and use it when you need it, reducing outages, protecting critical operations, and improving energy independence.

Utility-Scale & Minigrid Solutions
