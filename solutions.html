Commercial Businesses

For office complexes, retail stores, supermarkets, banks and corporate facilities, unreliable power and rising diesel costs cut directly into productivity and margins. LPVT Solutions designs commercial systems around your real operating hours, not a generic package.

We assess your daytime and after-hours load separately, then size a solar or hybrid solar-battery system that keeps critical operations — POS systems, refrigeration, lighting, HVAC and IT infrastructure — running without interruption.

Backed by our own manufacturing facility in Lagos, remote monitoring lets us track system performance and uptime after installation, and flexible financing options mean you can go solar without a large upfront outlay.

Homes & Residential Estates

As Nigeria's power situation continues to push households toward generators, LPVT Solutions designs home solar systems that give households, apartments, gated communities and luxury residences full backup power without the noise, fuel costs, or maintenance headaches of a generator.

Every home system starts with a load audit of what you actually run — lighting, fridges, air conditioning, pumps, entertainment systems — so your solar and battery capacity is sized correctly rather than oversold or underpowered.

Backed by our own manufacturing facility in Lagos, we install, commission and hand over your system with a 25-year panel warranty and 10-year battery warranty, and remain on call for monitoring and support long after installation.

Hospitality & Leisure

For hotels, resorts, restaurants, lounges, serviced apartments and event facilities, downtime and diesel costs eat directly into guest experience and margins. LPVT Solutions engineers systems built for round-the-clock operation.

Our engineering team carries out full site assessments and load modelling before design, then builds solar and battery capacity, with hybrid switching that works alongside your existing generators, so rooms stay comfortable and service standards stay consistently high through peak periods.

Because we manufacture our own panels in Lagos, we maintain tighter control over lead times, quality, and warranty support than providers who import third-party equipment.

Communities & Infrastructure

Many communities, estates and public infrastructure projects across Nigeria operate with little or no reliable grid connection. LPVT Solutions builds mini-grids, smart streetlighting, and community-scale energy systems that don't depend on the grid at all.

We start with a community or site-wide energy needs assessment, then design a solar and storage system with distribution built for shared or multi-building sites — expanding access to reliable power while improving security, connectivity and economic activity.

Backed by local manufacturing, engineering expertise, and proven project delivery capabilities, our installation and commissioning teams handle deployment nationwide, with lifecycle maintenance and technical support so the infrastructure keeps serving the community long after handover.
