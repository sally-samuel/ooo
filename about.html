Our Story

Helping Nigerians build a more reliable energy future

LPVT Solutions is the energy solutions arm of LPV Technologies, combining solar manufacturing, engineering expertise, project delivery, and long-term technical support through one accountable partner.

What makes us different is our connection to local manufacturing. Operating alongside one of Nigeria's leading solar panel manufacturing facilities in Lagos, we maintain greater control over quality, availability, technical support, and warranty management — giving our customers faster response times and greater accountability.

We design and deliver solar and energy storage solutions for businesses, industries, institutions, communities, and homes, providing everything from energy assessment and system design to installation, commissioning, monitoring, and lifecycle support.

Beyond the solutions we deliver, we believe Nigeria's energy transition should create lasting local value through manufacturing, skills development, job creation, and stronger renewable energy capabilities.

Why We Exist

Energy powers everything you do

From businesses and factories to hospitals, schools, estates, and homes, reliable electricity is essential to productivity, growth, security, and everyday life.

Yet for many Nigerians, unreliable grid supply, rising diesel costs, and generator dependence continue to increase operating expenses and create uncertainty.

We help our customers achieve reliable power, lower energy costs, and greater energy independence through intelligently designed solar and energy storage solutions built for long-term performance.
